# ADR-008: Product Bible v1.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-07

## Context

Jimvio accumulated comprehensive documentation: Product Blueprint, System/Technical Architecture, Operating Model, Feature Specs, Design System, ADRs, and governance principles. Leadership lacked a single **executive handbook** that a new investor, executive, engineer, or designer could read to fully understand Jimvio.

## Problem Statement

How do we ensure every future product, engineering, design, AI, and business decision aligns with a permanent source of truth?

## Decision

Create `docs/product-bible/JIMVIO-PRODUCT-BIBLE-v1.0.md` as the **Product Bible** — 10 parts:

1. Company (vision, values, culture, decisions)
2. Product (philosophy, personas, positioning, advantages)
3. Platform (all modules)
4. AI (complete strategy)
5. Engineering (references governance stack)
6. Design (tokens, a11y, components)
7. Commerce (products through subscriptions)
8. Creator Economy (communities through reputation)
9. Operations (deploy, security, continuity)
10. Roadmap (12/24/36/60 months)

The Bible **synthesizes** companion docs; deep specs remain in Blueprint/Architecture/Specs.

**Alignment rule:** All decisions must align with the Bible; updates require CPO + CTO + Founder.

## Consequences

### Positive impacts
- Onboarding for any role in one document
- Investor/executive narrative
- Decision alignment anchor

### Trade-offs
- Must stay synchronized with companion docs quarterly
- Bible is narrative; specs remain authoritative for implementation detail
