## What this is

The **Product Bible** is Jimvio's executive handbook — the single document that explains **who we are, what we build, why we win, and where we're going**. Every future feature, engineering decision, design decision, AI integration, business decision, and roadmap must align with it.

## Alignment rule

> Before any decision — product, engineering, design, AI, or business — ask: **Does this align with the Product Bible?**  
> If no, reject or update the Bible through formal review (CPO + CTO + Founder).
