# Incident Response

## Severity
- SEV1: security event, data loss, or complete customer outage.
- SEV2: major workflow unavailable or material financial risk.
- SEV3: degraded feature with a practical workaround.
- SEV4: minor defect or operational task.

## Roles
Incident commander owns decisions and timeline. Technical lead investigates and mitigates. Communications lead updates stakeholders. Scribe records evidence, decisions, and follow-up actions.

## Communication templates
**Initial:** “We are investigating an issue affecting [scope] since [time]. Next update by [time].”

**Update:** “Mitigation is [status]. Customer impact is [scope]. Next update by [time].”

**Resolved:** “The incident affecting [scope] is resolved. We will publish corrective actions after review.”

Run a blameless review for SEV1–2 incidents within five business days.
